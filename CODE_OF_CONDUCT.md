# Community participation

Use respectful, specific feedback. Explain the observed behavior and desired outcome. Preserve the context of other people's contributions and acknowledge corrections through the relevant issue, discussion or review.

Keep credentials, private repository data and personal information out of public reports. Report security vulnerabilities through the private channel in SECURITY.md. Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content.

Maintainers use GitHub's moderation tools when needed to protect participants and remove exposed private information. Decisions should retain an appropriate explanation and follow the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
